CS 227 — Interactive Readings

Short readings with activities you work as you go — one per class session. Each activity gives instant, targeted feedback (with answer options shuffled each visit), and your progress is saved in this browser. Every reading ends with an Exam practice section (easy / medium / challenge) for review.

Built to sit alongside the class notes (book.tex), the guided-inquiry activities, and the in-class problem sets. Readings are consolidation and practice — assign each one after its class activity so it doesn’t pre-empt the in-class discovery. Logical connectives appear only after they’re introduced in class (∀/∃/∧/→ from Reading 6; negation from 15; ∨, DeMorgan, and proof by cases from 16; contradiction from 17). Structural induction (11) precedes induction on ℕ (12). The Exam-practice sections (384 problems in all) are not graded and don’t affect the progress bar.
